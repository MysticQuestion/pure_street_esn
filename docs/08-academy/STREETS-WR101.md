# STREETS-WR101 — Oakland Waste & Recycling Certification

Course version: `WR101-2026.08`  
Last reviewed: 2026-08-29 against Oakland Recycles (sorting guide dated Dec 2025)  
Jurisdiction: City of Oakland, California  
Live: [oaklandstreets.live/academy](https://oaklandstreets.live/academy)

STREETS Academy is independent civic education. It is not a City of Oakland system, not a hauler training program, and does not replace Oakland Recycles, OAK311, Waste Management, California Waste Solutions, or StopWaste. Field observations are not official contamination determinations.

## Purpose

Participants learn to:

- identify Oakland’s major waste streams
- distinguish recycling, compost, trash, hazardous waste, bulky waste, and specialty materials
- recognize contamination
- understand illegal dumping and its prevention
- map first pathways: WM, CWS, OAK311, StopWaste, property owners, residents
- recognize environmental and public-health consequences of improper disposal
- document conditions without inventing blame
- apply California organics-diversion fundamentals (SB 1383 / ORRO) in plain language

## Experience

Interactive certification, not a slide deck. About 60–90 minutes online. Optional field practicum 60–120 minutes.

### Module notes

1. **Away is not a place.** Collection, sorting, processing, compost, recycle, reuse, disposal. One wrong sort can contaminate a recoverable load. Prevention sits above disposal.
2. **Oakland recycling grades:** paper, plastic bottles/jugs/tubs, metal, glass. Clean / empty / dry. Recycle plastics by shape, not chasing-arrows number. No bags, film, foam, food, tanglers, or hazardous material in recycling. Residential recycling collector: California Waste Solutions.
3. **Oakland compost** is not “anything biodegradable.” Compostable plastics, PLA, plastic-lined cups, bags, diapers, and pet waste stay out.
4. **Trash is residual**, not default. Run the four-question test before landfilling.
5. **Special materials:** batteries, paint, chemicals, lamps, e-waste, sharps, automotive fluids, propane. Pathway: Alameda County HHW. Observe → document → distance → refer.
6. **Bulky vs dumping.** WM bulky: 1-888-WM-BULKY; drop-off at Davis Street Resource Recovery Complex with proof of Oakland residency; curbside by appointment. Never assign origin without evidence.
7. **System map (residential):** CWS recycling; WM trash and compost; City of Oakland / 311 public right-of-way; StopWaste / ORRO; property owners; residents. STREETS is not an official actor in this stack.
8. **Contamination levels 0–3** are STREETS field classification, not an official determination.
9. **ORRO / SB 1383** in plain language, adapted to the learner’s role (resident, property manager, restaurant, BID, school, workforce, municipal staff).
10. **Hierarchy:** refuse, reduce, reuse, recycle/compost, special disposal, landfill last.
11. **Justice as measurement:** recurrence, duration, access, exposure, lawful disposal options — not slogans.
12. **Field method:** 10-step observation protocol; fact vs inference; miniature incident record; preparation for the 98th Avenue simulation.

## Certification simulation — 98th Avenue

Locked until all twelve modules are complete. Ten conditions on a simulated East Oakland block. Score dimensions: classification, disposal, safety, routing, documentation, evidence. Pass at 80/100.

## Independence and ethics

- Conditions, not people.
- No biometric identification, person tracking, encampment targeting, or person-based prediction.
- Observe, document, maintain distance, refer — especially around hazardous material and sharps.
- A published conflict-of-interest firewall separates STREETS evidence from Pure Street commercial services.
- Possible future partner courses (WMAC, StopWaste, City of Oakland, CWS) must not be represented as partnerships until an agreement exists. None are claimed here.
