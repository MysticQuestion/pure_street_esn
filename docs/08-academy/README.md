# STREETS Academy

Independent civic education for Oakland waste literacy.

**Live product:** [https://oaklandstreets.live/academy](https://oaklandstreets.live/academy)

STREETS Academy is free civic education for the people who meet Oakland’s waste system: residents, property managers, shop floors, owners, and field trainees. Enroll in WR101 — twelve modules, four labs, 98th Avenue simulation, two-year verifiable certificate. Complements Oakland Recycles and OAK311. It does not replace them. It is not a City of Oakland system, not a hauler training program, and is not represented as a municipal credential.

Landing pitch: *Know the system. Change the street.* The curb is learnable. The first sort is the decision that matters.
